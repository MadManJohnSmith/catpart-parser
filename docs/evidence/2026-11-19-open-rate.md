## 3. Classification rule

For each row the probe calls `build_export(path, <tmp>/part.ply)` and reads the returned summary:

1. **MESH** — `summary["triangle_count"] > 0`. This is the facet store, the exact `a5 03 34` surface route (two-lane and subtype-4 patch heads), or the structured-grid route; the export priority is unchanged.
2. **DISPLAY** — `triangle_count == 0` and `summary["no_surface_display"]` has `point_count > 0` or `curve_segment_count > 0`. This is the shipped display block, read as-is; the probe does not re-decode points or segments.
3. **BLANK** — neither. The export either failed with a named `ExportError` or returned a vertices-only summary with no display block.

A BLANK classification is therefore stronger than "no triangles": the part has no decoded point run, no pool point, no law-holding `33 3X` record and no count-framed f32 array to show either. The BLANK list below verifies that directly with a bare `display.scan_part` pass (no spans, no pool): every one of the 56 has zero scan points and zero arrays.

## 7. What this does and does not say

- **It says the viewer has something to draw for 1,386 of 1,442 rows.** It does not say 1,386 parts have a surface. 223 of them are explicitly no-surface displays: zero triangles, decoded carriers only.
- **The 223 display rows do not pass L2** and cannot: the gate fires on `triangle_count < 1`. The real open rate is not an L2 rate and must not replace it in `fitness/history.jsonl`.
- **The 13 mesh-bearing L2 failures do open.** Eight are invariant failures (`not-manifold`, `degenerate-triangles`); five are the recovered surfaces the report predates. Whether their invariants hold is the separate L2 question.
- **The two levers compose on this corpus.** The bag-rep rows and the subtype-4 rows are disjoint; the measured combined run reproduces both deltas exactly. That is a result of this run, not an assumption of it.
- **The display route is not a catch-all.** 56 rows have nothing decodable to show, and the probe measures that emptiness rather than inferring it from an error class.
- **The report is prior to both commits.** `20261119T065149Z-e0016f` was generated at 06:51:49Z. The cross is a before/after, not two independent runs of the same code.
- **No source was modified.** The probe only calls the shipped `build_export`; the CSV's triangles and points come from its summary, not from a second decoder.
