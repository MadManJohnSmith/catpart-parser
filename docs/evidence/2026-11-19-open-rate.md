## 2. Classification rule

For each row the probe calls `build_export(path, <tmp>/part.ply)` and reads the returned summary:

1. **MESH** — `summary["triangle_count"] > 0`. This is the facet store, the exact `a5 03 34` surface route, or the structured-grid route; the export priority is unchanged.
2. **DISPLAY** — `triangle_count == 0` and `summary["no_surface_display"]` has `point_count > 0` or `curve_segment_count > 0`. This is the shipped display block, read as-is; the probe does not re-decode points or segments.
3. **BLANK** — neither. The export either failed with a named `ExportError` or returned a vertices-only summary with no display block.

A BLANK classification is therefore stronger than "no triangles": the part has no decoded point run, no pool point and no law-holding `33 3X` record to show either.

## 6. What this does and does not say

- **It says the viewer has something to draw for 1,367 of 1,436 rows.** It does not say 1,367 parts have a surface. 209 of them are explicitly no-surface displays: zero triangles, decoded carriers only.
- **The 209 display rows do not pass L2** and cannot: the gate fires on `triangle_count < 1`. The real open rate is not an L2 rate and must not replace it in `fitness/history.jsonl`.
- **The 8 mesh-bearing L2 failures do open.** They are counted in MESH because triangles exist; whether their invariants hold is the separate L2 question the report already answers.
- **The display route is not a catch-all.** 69 rows have nothing decodable to show; the five `no-evaluable-surface` rows are held back deliberately by the a5 priority, not by a missing decoder for points.
- **The report is prior to the display commit.** `20261119T065149Z-e0016f` was generated at 06:51:49Z; `b3b4a8d` landed at 07:38:47Z. The report's `untriangulable-grid` (16) and `empty-mesh` (20) classes are reproduced as DISPLAY, and its `missing-geometry-store` / `undecoded-variant` classes split into DISPLAY and BLANK. The cross is a before/after, not two independent runs of the same code.
- **No source was modified.** The probe only calls the shipped `build_export`; the CSV's triangles and points come from its summary, not from a second decoder.

```bash
export LD_LIBRARY_PATH=/usr/lib:${LD_LIBRARY_PATH:-}
export TMPDIR=/home/alan/.cache/t3-tmp
```

The probe writes `docs/evidence/data/open-rate-files.csv` (1,436 rows: `sha16, path, split, report class, open kind, triangles, points, segments`) in deterministic order (`sha256`, then `path`) and prints the overall, per-split, cross-class and BLANK tables above. The PLY goes to a temporary directory and is discarded; the corpus is opened read-only. A full run over the 3.4 GB corpus is a few minutes.
