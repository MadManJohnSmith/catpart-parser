## 2. Record layout (byte-measured)

Every curve record = a 7-B header + family + payload, tiling header-to-family (C1, `2026-11-10-composite-space.md`):

- Header: tag_a at `+0x00 (0d)`, tag_b at `+0x01 (1d)`, ident u16le at `+0x02 (2d)`, `00` at `+0x04 (4d)`, payload length u16le at `+0x05 (5d)`; family `33 3X` at `+0x07 (7d)`; payload from `+0x09 (9d)`.

Canonical families (byte-verified corpus-wide, skip-42 walk — §2.1):

| Family | Canonical len | Payload layout | Records walked (corpus) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `33 32` line | 2 | 40-B f32le block AFTER the family bytes at `+0x09 (9d)`: floats `[f0..f9]`, G2 reading `[P][Q][P]+|Q|` | 296,895 L2 / 508,138 corpus (`C01_line_walked`) |
| `33 33` mid-ring circle | 26 | 6 BE f32 `(cx, cy, cz, cos t, sin t, r)` + 2-B trailer at `+0x21 (33d)` | 38,298 L2 / 54,828 corpus (`C08_midring_walked`) |
| `33 37` circle | 18 | 4 BE f32 `(cx, cy, cz, r)` + 2-B trailer at `+0x19 (25d)` | 69,746 L2 / 119,107 corpus |
| `33 38` conic | 30 | 7 BE f32 `(cx, cy, cz, cos t, sin t, a, b)` + 2-B trailer at `+0x25 (37d)` | 5,920 L2 / 15,768 corpus |
| `33 36` | 2 | Out of slice: canonical len==2 with no tiling/header-follow evidence for a 40-B block. Measured for the exclusion claim (`C36_block_read`): 27,257 of 875,398 walked corpus records fail a 10-finite-f32le read at `+0x09 (9d)` (848,141 pass = 96.9% — a finite read alone does not establish a block form); undecoded | — |

2.1 **The `33 32` block position.** The canonical `33 32` record (header len == 2) is followed by a 40-B f32le block at `+0x09 (9d)` from the family bytes — i.e. the block overlaps the header-declared 2-B payload region. Evidence: (a) with the resume-past-block walk, 508,086 of the 508,138 walked canonical corpus records decode as 10 finite f32le values (`C01_line_block_finite`; the 52 non-finite/out-of-scale block rejects); (b) the next record's 7-B header follows at `+0x2A (42d)` from the family bytes (hexdump, box anchor 0018baa8, 2026-11-17); (c) the G2 `[P][Q][P]` law fires bit-exact only under this alignment (51,002 records vs 414 at the +2-byte-payload alignment — `C01_line_prep_law` vs `C01_line_prep_shift2`).

## 3. Per-construct decode laws and rates

All rates are committed in [`br3-curves-claims.csv`](data/br3-curves-claims.csv) (per claim, per scope: `corpus` = 1,442, `l2-pass` = 1,089). Every figure below is a CSV row.

### TOP-07 Vertex — decoded
- Pool groups via the P1-P3 structural validator (`catpart.geometry.pool`): wrapper `10 24 04 ff ff 00 00 00` at `+0x00 (0d)`, sub-kind `01 06` at `+0x08 (8d)`, P1g count at `+0x0A (10d)` (u8; `ff` escapes to u32le at `+0x0B (11d)`), 15-B vertex records `[05 08 01][f32le xyz]` from `+0x0B (11d)`.
- `T07_pool_count_law`: 1,927/1,927 = 1.0000 (L2) / 2,326/2,326 (corpus) — every pool group walks exactly to its count field.
- Detector honesty (§4 also carries it): the pool validator does NOT discriminate vertex identity, ordering or duplicates — it validates the structural walk (wrapper shape, count field, record walk) only. Synthetic negative: `test_vertex_pool_negative_count_mismatch`.

### TOP-06 Edge — decoded (grammar)
- Wrapped groups: wrapper + sub-kind `01 02` at `+0x08 (8d)` + P1g record count + records `02 <n> <n refs>` (record opcode `02` at `+0x00 (0d)`, n at `+0x01 (1d)`, refs from `+0x02 (2d)`; u8/u16be width by exact end-alignment against the next wrapper or store end).
- Unwrapped lists: `01 01 <count>` at `+0x00 (0d)` + the same record grammar ending exactly on a wrapper.
- End-alignment residue: groups that do not land exactly under either width are the E1 residue (named, not silently absorbed): strict scanning raises `malformed-edge-group`, the triage script scans leniently and reports `edge_groups_ok` vs `edge_groups`.

### CRV-01 Line/Segment — decoded (layout); field semantics open (named)
- Layout: §2.1. `C01_line_block_finite` (l2): 296,853/296,895 = 0.9999 over walked canonical records (42 non-finite/out-of-scale block rejects on the L2 population; corpus 508,086/508,138).
- The G2 semantic laws as MEASURED FLAGS, not decoder preconditions: `C01_line_prep_law` (l2) = 0.1187 (corpus 0.1004), `C01_line_len_law` (l2) = 0.1369 (corpus 0.1078) — the P-repeat triple lives at `+0x21 (33d)`..`+0x29 (41d)` record-relative and repeats the first P triple at `+0x09 (9d)`..`+0x11 (17d)`; the length float at `+0x2D (45d)` is over the Q triple at `+0x15 (21d)`..`+0x1D (29d)` (rel tol 1e-6).
- **Ambiguity named**: the two laws hold and fail on records of the SAME store (per-store P-rep rates spread 0..1, median 0.6 over the 926 stores with >= 4 full-block line records — `C01_store_prep_population`/`C01_store_prep_median`). The G2 `[P][Q][P]+|Q|` reading is therefore established on its worked example and on the law-satisfying subpopulation only; the roles of `f3..f9` in the remaining blocks are open. `decode_line` returns the flags; the triage script measures them.

### CRV-02 Circle/Arc — decoded
- Layout: 4 BE f32 `(cx, cy, cz, r)` + 2-B trailer; `r` may be negative (sign convention open).
- Decode law: finite center/radius, `r != 0` (`C02_circle_decode_ok` l2: 69,738/69,746 = 0.9999; 8 rejections under the direction-free structural check, row-derived).
- Independent geometric cross-check: some vertex-pool point lies ON the circle (`|dist(v, center) - |r|| <= 5e-3`): `C02_circle_pool_law` (l2) = 68,770/69,746 = 0.9860 over ALL walked `33 37` records (the row denominator — not only stores with a decoded pool). The 0.0140 residue is the no-pool-store binding gap (multi-group stores, G2 open question 1), not a law failure — it is measured, not massaged.

### CRV-03 Ellipse (33 38 conic) — decoded (layout + direction law)
- Layout: 7 BE f32 `(cx, cy, cz, cos t, sin t, a, b)` + 2-B trailer.
- **BR3 direction law**: the in-plane direction pair `(cos t, sin t)` at `+0x15 (21d)`/`+0x19 (25d)` is unit-norm whenever both components are mid-range (tol 2e-3), and `(0, 0)` is the observed axis-aligned sentinel. `C03_conic_dir_law` (l2): 156/158 = 0.9873 mid-range payload pairs unit (corpus 5,406/5,408 = 0.9996; the counter is measured from the payload before the decode gate, so direction-law rejects stay in the denominator; label candidate — the structurally identical C08 row measures 0.9871). `C03_conic_decode_ok` (l2): 5,911/5,920 = 0.9985 (corpus 15,757/15,768 = 0.9993; the 11 corpus rejections split 7 short-payload + 2 direction-law + 2 b-positivity + 0 non-finite/out-of-scale — rows `C03_conic_rej_short`/`_dir`/`_b`/`_implausible`).
- **BR3 positivity law**: `b > 0` at `+0x21 (33d)` on 5,911/5,911 = 1.0000 (L2) / 15,757/15,757 (corpus).
- Circle-form records (`|a| == |b|`) are not separated by the decoder: the module does not assert the circle reading.
- Label: layout = established; the ellipse-axis reading of `(a, b)` = candidate (field-structure inference; no independent geometric cross-check landed in this slice).

### CRV-04 Parabola / CRV-05 Hyperbola — documented-open with ceiling arithmetic
- **Ceiling**: every parabola/hyperbola carrier must be a `33 38` conic record (the vocabulary's own signature): corpus ceiling = 357 files (L2 ceiling = 313 files, 28.74%) — the `C03_census_presence` row. The decoded 33 38 layout (15,768 corpus records) exposes NO eccentricity/type field at `+0x09 (9d)`..`+0x25 (37d)`: the 7 floats decode as center (finite, pool-scale), unit direction pair (5,406/5,408 = 99.96% of mid-range pairs; row `C03_conic_dir_law`), and `(a, b)` with `b > 0` always — no field separates e < 1 from e = 1 from e > 1.
- The 2-B trailer at `+0x25 (37d)` is un-decoded and is the only remaining candidate carrier; the 40-B f32le block hypothesis from stage-4a does NOT hold (no constant-size float block follows the canonical 30-B records).
- The frozen CSV's per-class counts (CRV-04 = 214, CRV-05 = 143 corpus files; note 214 + 143 = 357 exactly) have no committed generator (§6) and could not be reproduced from the bytes under any layout probe run in this slice.
- **Outcome**: documented-open. Recovery path: decode the 2-B trailer, or cross-check `33 38` records against CGMGeom conic payloads (BR5's slice).

### CRV-07 Polyline — decoded
- `01 02` edge groups with strip records `n > 2`: grammar as TOP-06. `C07_polyline_strips` (corpus) = 366/1,442; L2 = 330/1,089 = 0.3030 — the strict, end-aligned walk is narrower than the census's wrapped-group presence (0.8365 L2), which counts any `01 02` wrapper sub-kind regardless of record decode.
- Detector honesty: the width resolution (u8 vs u16be) is decided by exact end-alignment only; the element-space binding of the refs is NOT discriminated (composite numbering, F8-corrected).

### CRV-08 Trimmed Curve — candidate
- Evidence landed: `33 33` records carry an in-plane direction pair + radius (BR3 re-read of the stage-4a `p1`/`p2` params — dominant values `(0, 0)`, `(+-1, 0)`, `(0, +-1)`, mid-range unit pairs; `C08_midring_dir_law` l2 = 37,844/38,298 = 0.9881, corpus 54,118/54,828 = 0.9871), `33 38` records carry the `(a, b)` axis pair + direction; `33 37` records carry NO trim parameters (full-circle form). Parameter-bound SEMANTICS (angles, arc extent) stay open — the stage-4a "arcs splitting at 90/270 degrees" observation remains the only semantic anchor.
- The frozen `CRV-08` count (1,280/1,442 corpus, 0.975 L2) has no committed generator; my presence row (`C08_trim_presence`: files with any 33 33 or canonical 33 38 record) measures 969/1,442 corpus / 874/1,089 L2 — NOT equal to the frozen count, reported as-is.

## 4. Detector honesty (per class)

- **Vertex pool (`TOP-07`)**: validates the structural walk only (wrapper shape, count field, record walk) — does NOT discriminate vertex identity, ordering or duplicates. Synthetic negative: `test_vertex_pool_negative_count_mismatch`.
- **Curve-record walker**: does NOT discriminate store sections, topology consumers (edge vs tessellation plate vs mid-ring), or compressed-stream pseudo-records beyond the header/length/value plausibility cap. Synthetic negative: `test_walker_skips_garbage_header` (implausible header length does not absorb the walk), `test_walker_skips_non_finite_circle` (non-finite payload does not enter the walk).
- **`decode_line` (`33 32`)**: does NOT enforce the G2 `[P][Q][P]+|Q|` reading (measured flags — §3 CRV-01 ambiguity); does not discriminate the segment orientation convention. Synthetic negatives: `test_line_negative_bad_length` (length-law flag fires), `test_line_negative_non_finite_block` (structural rejection).
- **`decode_circle` (`33 37`)**: does NOT discriminate full circle vs arc (no trim parameters in the payload). Synthetic negatives: `test_circle_negative_zero_radius`, `test_circle_negative_nan_center`, `test_circle_negative_short_payload` (payload below the 18-B layout).
- **`decode_midring` (`33 33`)**: does not discriminate arc extent or the radius sign convention (negative radii observed). Synthetic negatives: `test_midring_negative_non_unit_direction`, `test_midring_negative_short_payload` (payload below the 26-B layout).
- **`decode_conic` (`33 38`)**: does NOT discriminate ellipse vs parabola vs hyperbola (the documented-open §3 result); `|a| == |b|` circle-form is not asserted. Synthetic negatives: `test_conic_negative_non_unit_direction`, `test_conic_negative_non_positive_b`, `test_conic_negative_short_payload` (payload below the 30-B layout); sentinel acceptance: `test_conic_zero_direction_sentinel_accepts`.
- **`decode_edge_group`**: does not discriminate the element-space binding of refs (F8-corrected: the full merged space, not only the pool). Synthetic negatives: `test_edge_group_negative_misaligned_end` (strict raises / lenient skips), width discrimination via `test_edge_group_u8_walk` / `test_edge_group_u16be_walk`.

## 6. Corrections and drift recorded

1. **2026-11-17 (BR3, this note): stage-4a `33 33` param reading superseded.** The `p1`/`p2` floats at `+0x15 (21d)`/`+0x19 (25d)` of the 26-B `33 33` payload are an in-plane unit direction pair `(cos t, sin t)` — the same pair shape as `33 38`'s — not curve parameters: dominant values `(0, 0)` (21,035 walked records; row `C08_midring_zero_sentinel`), `(+-1, 0)`/`(0, +-1)` (8,896; row `C08_midring_axis_pair`) and mid-range pairs (corpus-wide 10,051 of the 10,696 mid-range pairs unit — row `C08_midring_midrange_unit`; the 645 non-unit mid-range pairs sit in `decode_midring`'s 710 rejects; 5,406/5,408 is the `33 38` CONIC mid-range count). The stage-4a mid-ring law (z_lo <= cz <= z_hi, radius match) is unaffected.
2. **2026-11-17 (BR3, this note): G2 `33 32` law scope corrected.** The `[P][Q][P]+|Q|` law is established on the G2 small-store worked example (6/6) and on a corpus subpopulation (P-rep 10.04%, `|Q|` 10.78% corpus; skip-42 walk); it is NOT universal — both laws fail on records of the same store (per-store rates spread 0..1, median 0.6 corpus / 0.6042 L2 — rows `C01_store_prep_median`). G2 itself named this ("the family varies — semantics open, unknown 5"); BR3 pins the numbers (final walk: P-rep 51,002/508,138 = 10.04%, `|Q|` 54,775/508,138 = 10.78% corpus).
3. **2026-11-17 (BR3, this note): frozen-vocabulary generator drift.** `brep-construct-vocabulary.csv` (committed at df02b7d) carries per-construct rows (`TOP-06`, `CRV-01`..`CRV-05`, `CRV-07`, `CRV-08`) whose label set and per-class counts (`CRV-04` = 214, `CRV-05` = 143, `CRV-08` = 1,280) are not produced by the committed `census_brep_constructs.py` (which emits `GEOM_CURV_*` labels and one undifferentiated `GEOM_CURV_CONIC` class).
