# Evidence: the f32-run fallback's scope is the body blocks — 2026-11-20

**Status**: COMPLETE. Ticket #69 (`Part of #50`), on `main` @ `1f752f0`.
The empty-scan f32-run fallback (`display._f32_point_runs`, source label
`store-f32-points`) no longer reads the parametric part containers.

**Law**: the fallback scans the body/geometry blocks only. ``CATPrtCont``,
``CATProdCont`` and ``CATMechProdCont`` are the part tree and its feature
parameters; their f32 triples are tessellation cache and parameter arrays,
not a display carrier. The runs law was measured on body containers
(CATSeeBodyCont 57, CATBRepModeContainer 11, CATStdCont 9, CGMGeom 6 — 0 of
the 83 census files with a run in CATPrtCont). The gate drops those blocks
from the scan set, but every valid block still bounds its neighbours'
regions (``probe_region`` ends at the next valid block).

**Method**: `src/catpart/geometry/display.py`
(`F32_RUN_SKIP_BLOCKS`, `_raw_block_regions`) and the Rust twin
`port/src/geometry/display.rs`; measured with the shipped export seam
(`build_export`) over the 83 ``missing-store-census.csv`` rows that carry a
point run (the #60 acceptance set) and the 120-row no-facet population of
`grid-meshing-parts.csv`; Python=Rust parity through the release
`fidelity-cli`; the gate itself through `scripts/check.sh`. Read-only over
the corpus.

## McLaren Senna: 244,817 → 6 display points

`bench-external/datasets/mclaren/McLAREM SENNA 2019.CATPart` has 10 container
records and no store, no ``a5 03 34`` frame, no admitted ``e5`` record and no
facet. Every display point came from the fallback:

| Region | runs | points |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `CATPrtCont` `0xf3b3..0xe66e5a` (tessellation cache, 12-B f32 triples) | 679 | 244,811 |
| `CATSeeBodyCont` `0xe6d3a5..0xe6dea1` (the tolerance ladder `0xe6d62c` / `0xe6d820`) | 2 | 6 |

After the gate the part still opens as DISPLAY with the **6** `CATSeeBodyCont`
points (`sources` stays `store-f32-points`, PLY still empty). The two
``z = ±2048`` points are that tolerance ladder (1/32 … 2048), not a
fixed-point saturation — no numeric gate is added. The part's real geometry
(the 155,765 triples preceded by the CATIA id table, 96.7% within 0.5 mm of
the STEP) would need its own "tessellation-cache in `CATPrtCont`" law, not
this fallback.

## Measured effect

**#60 acceptance set (83 census files with a run, 2 container-less binaries
included)**: ungated vs gated export summaries are **identical on 83/83**
(`rows_losing_points = 0`, `rows_whose_summary_changed = 0`). The 16
run-only rows keep their exact counts, e.g. Crankshaft 4,114,
Ehi_BundleSegment1_2 2,842, Ehi_Structure 1,472, Ehi_Equipment 688,
NewScrew 230, CAAMaiUdfForMfgHoleInstance 254, Template 111.

**Official corpus (120 no-facet population)**: one part changes —
``cachete_soporte_mirror`` 27 → 18 points. Its 9 lost triples live inside
its ``CATPrtCont`` (`0x57b25`, `0xa737e`, `0xd8411`, 3 triples each) and are
byte patterns, not geometry: they push the cloud's bbox from ±128/±512/±2,048
to ±1,356/±15,724/±15,725 mm (the part is a knife support). Part classes are
unchanged (5 mesh, 105 display, 10 `undecoded-variant`); the population total
is 9,685 → **9,676** and `no-surface-fallback-parts.csv` /
`no-surface-fallback-claims.csv` (DF-02) are regenerated by the committed
probe.

**Parity**: Python vs Rust `fidelity-cli export` over the 83 census files +
the changed corpus part + McLaren — **85/85 summary-equal and PLY
byte-identical**.
