## 2. Invariants & Guarantees Upheld

- **P4 Assembly Bar Anti-Masking Invariant**: Undecodable and unresolved instances are NEVER placed at origin or defaulted to identity transforms. They are flagged with distinct status badges in the UI, omitted from spatial 3D bounding spheres, and carry `None`/`null` transforms.
- **Change of Basis Invariance**: $B = R_X(-\pi/2)$ transforms CATIA Z-up right-handed coordinates into Three.js Y-up right-handed coordinates with mathematical exactness and zero residuals.
- **GPU Resource Conservation**: Component geometries are decoded once and instanced across all assembly placements via `GeometryPool`.
- **Zero-Server Operation**: Entire ingestion, decoding, and rendering pipeline operates client-side in the browser via WebAssembly and WebGL.
