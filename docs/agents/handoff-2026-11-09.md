## Conventions that must not drift

- Bounded dispatch: one concrete deliverable, explicit STOP, partial
  results with the ambiguity named are acceptable
  (`docs/agents/dispatch-recipes.md`).
- Review per ticket before merge (delegate when the provider allows;
  coordinator fallback documented). Fixes for review findings are
  applied by the coordinator when mechanical.
- Linear history: rebase diverging branches, ff-only merges, tracker
  (`README.md`) + `Progress:` commit at every ticket close.
- Corpus binaries never committed; evidence notes + CSVs + committed
  decode scripts are the artifacts. Every claim byte-ranged with a
  reproduction command; corrections recorded, never massaged
  (C1-C11 so far).
- Suite stays corpus-free: pytest -q (201 passing at handoff), ruff
  clean, `check_format_claims` pinned by tests.
