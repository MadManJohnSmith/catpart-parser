# catpart-parser v1.0

**Release notes · 2026-11-18 · Apache-2.0**

## What this is

`catpart-parser` is a free, open-source reader for Dassault Systèmes CATIA V5
`.CATPart` and `.CATProduct` binaries. It reads the binary format directly — no
CATIA installation, no licence, no server — and ships two implementations kept
in lockstep by a fidelity harness: a Python reference (`catpart-inspect`,
`catpart-export`) and a Rust→WASM port that decodes and renders parts and
assemblies entirely in the browser. It renders the **decoded tessellation**, and
that decode is partial (see [Viewer fidelity](#viewer-fidelity-measured-2026-11-18)).

v1.0 is the terminal state defined in
[ADR-0004](https://github.com/MadManJohnSmith/catpart-parser/blob/main/docs/adr/0004-terminal-state-web-viewer.md):
every phase is closed, unreachable results are recorded as documented
negatives, the corpus definition is frozen, and the fitness record is
append-only. This release closes Phase 7 (B-rep) and records the L3 level in
the fitness history for the first time.

The project stays public and usable; **no maintenance is promised**. A later
bugfix may ship as `v1.0.1` without reopening the project.

- Live viewer (client-side WebAssembly):
  <https://madmanjohnsmith.github.io/catpart-parser/viewer/viewer.html>
- Docs site: <https://madmanjohnsmith.github.io/catpart-parser/>

## Corpus & method

- **1,824 unique files** — 1,442 parts and 382 products — split **Dev 1,751 /
  held-out 73**
  ([corpus report](https://github.com/MadManJohnSmith/catpart-parser/blob/main/docs/corpus-report.md)).
- The corpus definition is
  [`manifests/corpus-manifest.csv`](https://github.com/MadManJohnSmith/catpart-parser/blob/main/manifests/corpus-manifest.csv),
  SHA-256 `dc75f8fe9156dd69b26f2a44ff642c396c7c484d4049a831b88cf6b91d11b164`.
  The same digest is the `corpus_digest` of every committed fitness row, so the
  corpus definition has not moved since the first accepted run.
- **Freeze statement — what was verified.** This release verifies the
  **manifest**, not the binaries on disk. A fitness run validates the
  manifest's required fields (per-file SHA-256 format, kind, split, path, no
  duplicate digests) and never re-hashes `corpus/`; the binaries are not
  redistributed. Verified backup of 2026-11-07 stands.
- **Method.** Every decode law is reverse-engineered from the observed corpus
  and recorded in dated evidence notes with byte ranges, per-claim data CSVs and
  cited scripts. Negative results are recorded as first-class outcomes. The
  fitness protocol (ADR-0001) appends one row per run and never edits or
  reorders a previous row; a rejected row stays as the record.
- **L3 denominator.** L3 is construct coverage over the 23-row vocabulary frozen
  by the Phase 7 census, not a corpus-wide exact-solid rate. It has no
  Dev/held-out split because construct coverage is a property of the committed
  evidence set, not of a corpus sample.
- 1,818 of the 1,824 unique files are genuine CATIA V5 containers. The six
  `magic_ok=no` files are mislabeled non-binaries kept for provenance; they are
  the six non-clean L1 Dev parses, not parser bugs.

## What is decoded vs closed

All 23 frozen constructs are accounted for. "Decoded" means a byte layout or
recognition law, a corpus rate, tests and a synthetic negative. Depth is not
uniform: the three curved surface rows are decoded at tessellation level,
`TOP-03` Shell as a derived partition law, and `SRF-01` as a record-local plane;
each claim row states its scope.

| Family | Decoded (14) | Documented negative (3) | Documented open (6) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Topology (7)** | `TOP-01` Solid/Body, `TOP-03` Shell, `TOP-04` Face, `TOP-05` Loop, `TOP-06` Edge, `TOP-07` Vertex | `TOP-02` Lump | — |
| **Curves (8)** | `CRV-01` Line/Segment, `CRV-02` Circle/Arc, `CRV-03` Ellipse (conic-level), `CRV-07` Polyline | `CRV-06` NURBS curve | `CRV-04` Parabola, `CRV-05` Hyperbola, `CRV-08` Trimmed curve |
| **Surfaces (8)** | `SRF-01` Plane, `SRF-02` Cylindrical, `SRF-03` Conical, `SRF-04` Spherical | `SRF-08` NURBS surface | `SRF-05` Toroidal, `SRF-06` Revolution, `SRF-07` Extruded |

The two rows that were still `candidate` in the pre-adjudication record —
`CRV-08` and `SRF-07` — were adjudicated on 2026-11-18 as documented open with
committed ceiling rows, which is why the candidate count is 0
(`BR6L3-30`).

## Documented negatives and opens

### Parametric recipe recovery

Features, sketches and editable parameters are **out of scope by decision**. No
parametric rebuild is claimed; the Phase 3 specification-tree negative stands
(ROADMAP Phase 7).

### Products carry no geometry store

The Phase 6 census verified **0/382** product rows with a geometry store. An
earlier 0/37 figure was a sample; it is not the census result. The assembly
viewer resolves structure, instance placement and transforms — not product
geometry.

### The L2 ceiling is measured, not unfinished

74.80% is the reachable ceiling, established class by class (ROADMAP Phase 4
close):

- 128 no-pool stores carry zero facet records — structurally incapable of an
  export.
- 125 missing-locator headers are unreachable by construction.
- The UV family's element coordinates are not in the payload.
- The remaining not-manifold failures are content fins and coplanar books
  present in the bytes.
- The measured residual ceiling (+32) landed in the final stage; the 80% bar was
  re-adjusted to **74.80% at margin 0** under the pre-authorized rule, with the
  arithmetic published.

### The three L3 documented negatives

- **`TOP-02` Lump** — no record-level detector exists and the census emits no
  lump label; the nearest label (`TOPO_BODY`) cannot separate bodies from lumps.
  The published ceiling (`BR5-06b_top02_lump_ceiling`) is the framed subset of
  the CGM region — 50,486,667 of 151,999,604 bytes (0.3321) — because no lump
  population is isolated.
- **`CRV-06` NURBS curve / `SRF-08` NURBS surface** — no degree/knot/control-point
  record class is isolated; content signatures find 0/1,089 files. Their 1,435
  census figure is CGM presence, not NURBS content
  (`BR5-06d_crv06_srf08_ceiling`).

### The six L3 documented opens, with ceilings

- **`CRV-04` Parabola / `CRV-05` Hyperbola** — no parabola/hyperbola
  discriminator is decoded. Ceiling: the **357** files carrying any `33 38`
  conic carrier (**313** on the L2-pass population). The frozen conic split is
  not a partition of a decoded family: the two rows share that same carrier set
  (`C04_parabola_ceiling`, `C05_hyperbola_ceiling`).
- **`CRV-08` Trimmed curve** — a trimmed curve is indistinguishable from an
  untrimmed one in the decoded fields. Ceiling: **1,277/1,442** files
  (0.8856; 1,086/1,089 on L2-pass) carrying any walked `33 3X` record, and
  **1,573,239** records (`C08_trim_curve_ceiling`,
  `C08_trim_curve_ceiling_records`). The narrower 969-file carrier reading is a
  reading, not the ceiling.
- **`SRF-05` Toroidal / `SRF-06` Revolution** — a torus band is a valid cylinder
  at either equator and planar at the pole, so no class population is a torus
  population. Ceiling: the full classified strip-loop total, **67,919** loops
  across **975/1,442** files (`SRF-05_upper_bound_classified_patches`,
  `SRF-05_upper_bound_allscopes_presence`).
- **`SRF-07` Extruded** — the `pencil-noncyl` signature isolates only
  non-circular profile extrusions; a circular-profile extrusion **is** a
  cylinder. The **5,517** loops in **388** files are a **floor**, never a bound;
  the published bound is the same 67,919 superset
  (`SRF-07_extrusion_ceiling`).

## Viewer fidelity (measured 2026-11-18)

**v1.0 is not a faithful visual reproduction of a CATPart.** The viewer renders
the decoded tessellation exactly; it does not invent or smooth anything. The
gap is in what the decode recovers, and L2 "pass" certifies topology legality
(non-empty, manifold, plausible bounds, no degenerate triangles) — **not surface
completeness**. A headless audit of four corpus files measured:

- `Gabarit_trou_alu_alpha.CATPart` (L2 pass): 4,420 triangles, **402 mm² of
  surface inside a ~12,500 mm² envelope (~3%)** — only the hole rims are
  present; the plate body is absent.
- `labios/main.CATPart` (L2 pass): 4,261 triangles, **2,299 mm² of ~5,600 mm²
  (~41%)** — a visibly hollow body.
- `SP02_BETA_COIFFE.CATPart`: 424 vertices, **0 triangles** — the viewer showed
  a blank canvas as a successful load (fixed after this audit: `empty-mesh` is
  now a failure state).
- `CAAMmrPartCopy_Comp1.CATPart`: no vertex pool — fails with a named error, as
  reported.

Across the 1,436 parts, **321 (22.4%) produce no triangles at all**: 144
`undecoded-variant` (no pool), 119 `missing-geometry-store`, 58 `empty-mesh`
(vertices only). Of the 58, 55 carry **no facet record at all**: the CGM block
is an identity/object dictionary with zero NURBS coordinate payload (BR5,
re-measured), so no surface carrier the project decodes exists in those files —
they store edges/curves, and up to ~2.9 MB per file of stream data remains
unaccounted. Of the 144 `undecoded-variant` parts, 128 have no `01 06` vertex
pool anywhere and 16 carry real facets (2 recoverable by a cheap arithmetic
law, 1 by a refs law, 13 behind the unsolved packing law with a committed
ceiling of ~0 payoff). Of the parts that do produce a mesh, coverage varies and
is not currently measured by the fitness protocol.

Consequence: treat the viewer as a **partial decode inspector**, not a CAD
viewer. Closing this gap is the reopened post-v1.0 work
([#50](https://github.com/MadManJohnSmith/catpart-parser/issues/50)); it requires
a surface-coverage metric in L2, the missing-face families in the passing
parts, and eventually the exact-model payload (currently a documented negative).

## Honest limitations

- **Eight frozen denominator cells have no committed generator.** `CRV-04`,
  `CRV-05`, `CRV-08`, `SRF-03`, `SRF-04`, `SRF-05`, `SRF-06` and `SRF-07` are
  quoted as frozen input, never as decode rates: 15 of the 23 counts reproduce
  exactly from the committed census CSV, and these eight do not, nor are their
  rates expressible as `round(k/1089, 4)` for any integer `k`
  ([provenance audit](https://github.com/MadManJohnSmith/catpart-parser/blob/main/docs/agents/br6-vocabulary-provenance.md)).
  The L3 headline is row-level construct coverage and does not rest on those
  cells; corpus prevalence is reported alongside in the Phase 7 note and never
  gates. Re-freezing the denominator would shrink 23 → 18 rows and delete
  independently established outcomes, so it is kept and disclosed instead.
- **Four denominators are mis-scoped carriers, not decode rates.** `TOP-02`
  is body/CGM presence; `CRV-03` is every conic, not only ellipses; `CRV-06`
  and `SRF-08` are 1,435 = `cgm_present`, with content measured at 0/1,089.
  `CRV-03` = 357 also double-counts the `CRV-04` + `CRV-05` carrier set; the
  three are not a partition.
- **The L3 scorer verifies citations, not geometry at score time.** It resolves
  each construct to one committed claim row and validates the citation, path,
  label and attribution, and fails closed on a mismatch. The underlying decode
  evidence is in the claims CSVs and evidence notes; the scalar is coverage over
  that evidence.
- **Exact STEP was not achieved.** Exact-mode exports are **0/1,442** parts:
  **0/76** closed shells satisfy the loop-segment B-rep pairing law (76/76 carry
  a one-use segment), so the exporter refuses with `shell-not-brep-paired`
  rather than inventing topology (`BR6G-05b`, `BR6G-05c`, `BR6G-06a`).
- **Faceted STEP is the shipped STEP surface.** Facet mode exports **53/1,442**
  parts (`BR6G-07a`); it is the measured faceted subset, labelled as such in the
  STEP `FILE_DESCRIPTION`, not a precise B-rep claim.
- **STEP carries no product structure.** The exporter writes parts; assemblies
  are handled at viewer level. Product structure in STEP is not claimed.
- **Only 66/1,442** parts decode at least one closed shell (`BR6G-01`), and 283
  parts are `body-no-shell` (125 no-store + 128 face-free + 30 no-placeable);
  the graph refuses what it cannot place instead of emitting orphan geometry.
- **The corpus freeze is a manifest freeze.** The release verifies the manifest
  digest and its fields; it does not re-hash `corpus/`. Binaries are not
  redistributed.

## Reproduce

The viewer needs no install: open a `.CATPart`, or a `.CATProduct` with its
parts folder or `.zip`, at the
[live viewer](https://madmanjohnsmith.github.io/catpart-parser/viewer/viewer.html).

```console
$ git clone https://github.com/MadManJohnSmith/catpart-parser.git
$ cd catpart-parser
$ python -m venv .venv && . .venv/bin/activate
$ pip install '.[test,lint]'

$ catpart-inspect model.CATPart
$ catpart-export model.CATPart -o mesh.ply

# the full published record: requires the corpus binaries (never run in CI)
$ catpart-fitness --manifest manifests/corpus-manifest.csv \
    --corpus-root . --fitness-dir fitness

$ bash scripts/check.sh    # local CI mirror: ruff, pytest, Rust/WASM, node, Playwright
```

The Phase 7 evidence sequence (the BR1–BR6 triage scripts plus
`scripts/triage/score_l3_constructs.py`) is listed in §7 of the
[Phase 7 close note](https://github.com/MadManJohnSmith/catpart-parser/blob/main/docs/evidence/2026-11-18-phase7-close.md).
Reproducing the published scalar requires the same corpus bytes; the committed
manifest carries a per-file SHA-256 so a local corpus can be checked, and a
fitness run validates manifest fields without re-hashing the binaries.

## Post-release (2026-11-19)

After the `v1.0` tag, the viewer-fidelity work landed two decoder laws (per-loop
weld with a manifold guard; the extended-header facet family, gated on manifold
assembly), the `0x49` short-form family, the primary-emit seam guard and a
coordinate-duplicate dedup. **L2 Dev rose from 74.80% to 78.49%**
(1,084/1,381) and held-out from 56/61 to **57/61**; L1 and L3 are unchanged
(23/23). The accepted row is `20261119T035609Z-7dc3ea`.

The tag stays immutable; this section records the later measurements rather than
moving it. Two questions were also closed with evidence: the `_Position` layer
is the `8d81` stream (the identity header is a constraint frame; 8 real
transforms corrected, 0 regressions), and the CGM block **cannot** yield exact
solids (0/128 exact tiling, no numeric payload), so exact B-rep remains
open-ended bulk RE. Work continues under issue #50.
